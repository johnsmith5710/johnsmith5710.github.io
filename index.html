American-Made Since 1968

Lyons Industries is a third-generation, family-owned manufacturer in Dowagiac, Michigan. We produce more than 1,500 bathtubs and showers every day for homeowners, retailers, builders, and the RV industry — and every piece is built on the same five principles that have guided us for over 55 years:

Preparation, Respect, Integrity, Quality, and Service.

A Family Tradition

Lyons Industries was founded in 1968 by the Lyons family in Dowagiac, Michigan. What started with a single specialized plumbing vent for the mobile home industry has grown — through three generations of family leadership — into one of the country's most trusted manufacturers of residential and RV bathware.

We're proud to be part of the Dowagiac community, and proud that every product carrying the Lyons name is built here by our team.
