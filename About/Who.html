Who We Are

Three generations of family craftsmanship in Dowagiac, Michigan.

A Brief History of Lyons Industries

"My name is Lance Lyons and I'm president of Lyons Industries, Inc. My father and grandfather started the company in Dowagiac, Michigan in 1968. They developed a specialized plumbing vent for the mobile home industry. As the business grew, he bought his first vacuum former in the 1970s and started producing glue-up wall surrounds. Now, we produce more than 1,500 bathtubs and showers daily!"

— Lance Lyons, President
