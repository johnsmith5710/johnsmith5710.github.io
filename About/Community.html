Community Involvement

Giving back to the Dowagiac area we've called home since 1968.

Rooted in Dowagiac

Lyons Industries is a family-owned and family-oriented business that is dedicated to supporting and giving back to our community. Below are some of the ways we've supported Dowagiac and the surrounding area.

2022 Michigan Works! Employer of the Year

Lyons Industries was proud to be named the 2022 Michigan Works! Association Employer of the Year. This award recognizes companies that go above and beyond in supporting their employees and the broader workforce in Michigan.

We know we wouldn't have enjoyed the level of success we've had without our hardworking team, which is why we take extra effort to return the favor and set them up for long-term success.

Supporting Local Restaurants During the Pandemic

The COVID-19 pandemic brought restrictions and uncertainty for restaurants in the Dowagiac area. Lance Lyons was committed to helping local restaurants weather the storm, organizing meal purchases and finding ways to keep the doors open at the places that make Dowagiac feel like home.
