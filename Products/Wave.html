The Wave Line

Wave is our workhorse remodel line. Every wall surround fastens to the studs through a nail flange, so one crew can set a full tub or shower wall in a single visit. Four moulded shelves come standard.

Direct to Stud

The nail flange fastens to the framing. You skip the tile and the grout.

Four Shelves

Two back shelves and two side shelves come moulded into every wall surround.

Robotic Finish

Robotically sprayed fiberglass holds the same wall thickness on every part.

Built in Dowagiac

We mould, spray, and pack the Wave line in our own Michigan plant.

3-Piece

Wall Surrounds

Three-piece surrounds for tub walls and shower walls. Pick the 59 in. height over a bathtub or the 74 in. height over a shower base.

The three panels pass through a standard door and up a stairway. One installer can carry each piece.

Product image — Wall Surrounds
Shower Base

Single Threshold Shower Bases

A 60 in. by 32 in. base with a low single threshold and a slip resistant floor.

Drain position options let you match the base to the rough-in you already have.

Product image — Single Threshold Shower Bases
4-Piece

Seated Showers

A four-piece seated shower for bathrooms that need a place to sit. The seat moulds into the base.

The 78 in. height pairs with the same Wave wall panels and the same trim.
