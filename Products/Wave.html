Surrounds

Seven Sizes, One Clean Wall

Seven surrounds, a piece for any sized alcove. Five sit over a shower base. Two sit over a bathtub or a seated base.

Four shelves are already moulded in, and every panel clears a standard doorway and a stairwell.

Product image — Seven Sizes, One Clean Wall
Shower Bases

A Low Step and Sure Footing

Five shower bases with an easy step threshold and a floor that grips underfoot.

The 60 in. bases come with a right or a left drain. The smaller bases drain from the center.

Product image — A Low Step and Sure Footing
Seated Bases

A Place to Sit, Moulded In

The seat is part of the base. There is no add-on bench, no bracket to mount, and no seam to seal.

Seated bases take the same Wave surrounds and the same trim, so the finished bathroom still matches.
