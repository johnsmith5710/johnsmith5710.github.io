One Line, the Whole Bathroom

Elite is our widest line, and the Elite II surround is the new flagship. It covers bathtubs, shower bases, seated bases, and surrounds in both the 59 in. and the 69 in. height. Builders order it to a schedule, and it suits a single bathroom just as well.

The Elite II Flagship

Our newest surround leads the line in the 60 in. by 32 in. and 60 in. by 30 in. sizes.

Up to Six Shelves

Elite surrounds carry as many as six 3 in. shelves, and some add a shave shelf.

One Person Installation

Apply the adhesive and screw to the studs. One installer can set the wall.

Made in Michigan

We mould, spray, and pack every part in our own Dowagiac plant.

Surrounds

Two Heights, Nine Sizes

The 69 in. surrounds go over a shower base. The 59 in. surrounds go over a bathtub or a seated base.

Sizes run from 32 in. by 32 in. up to 60 in. by 34 in., so there is a wall for the opening you have.

Product image — Two Heights, Nine Sizes
Bases

Shower Bases and Seated Bases

Five shower bases cover the common rough-ins, in both standard floor and above floor rough.

Two seated bases add a moulded 18 in. seat for a bathroom that needs somewhere to sit.

Product image — Shower Bases and Seated Bases
Bathtubs

Three Bathtubs, Built to Last

Elite bathtubs come in 60 in. and 54 in. lengths, each with a right or a left drain.

Moulded lumbar support and arm rests make the tub comfortable, and a reinforced apron carries the load.
