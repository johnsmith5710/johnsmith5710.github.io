Built Around a Transfer Seat

Contour moulds an extra large 18 in. transfer seat into the base, and the curved front opens up the room to shower in while still fitting a standard alcove. Two surrounds cover the 30 in. and the 32 in. depth.

An 18 in. Transfer Seat

The extra large seat is part of the base and adds safety when moving into the shower.

A Curved Front

The curve gives more room to shower in and still fits a standard alcove.

Textured, Slip Resistant

The floor is textured across a spacious area for safer footing.

Made in Michigan

We mould, spray, and pack every part in our own Dowagiac plant.

Seated Base

A Seat You Can Transfer Onto

The 60 in. by 32 in. seated base carries an extra large 18 in. transfer seat, shaped to move onto from the side.

It comes with a right or a left drain, and the flange screws straight to the studs.

Surrounds

Walls That Fit the Base

Two surrounds cover the 30 in. and the 32 in. depth, each with three 4 in. shelves.

They also fit most standard seated bases and bathtubs already in place.
