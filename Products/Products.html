Residential

Bathtubs

Durable, easy-to-clean tubs in dozens of designs — from compact alcove installs to deep soaking tubs built for everyday comfort.

Available in alcove, drop-in, and freestanding configurations. Engineered for straightforward installation and long-term reliability.

Residential & Commercial

Wall Surrounds

Multi-piece wall surrounds that fasten straight to the studs or bond to the wall already in the room. A 59 in. height goes over a bathtub or a seated base; 69 in. and 74 in. go over a shower base.

Moulded shelves are already built in, and every panel clears a standard doorway — no grout, no tile, no guesswork.

Product image — Showers & Surrounds
Shower Bases

Single Threshold Shower Bases

Low single threshold bases with a textured, slip resistant floor. Sizes run from 32 in. by 32 in. up to 60 in. by 34 in. across the Wave, Elite, Garden, and Select ADA lines.

The 60 in. bases come with a right or a left drain, smaller bases drain from the center, and Select ADA enters at just 1 in.

Product image — Shower Bases
Seated Bathing

Seated Bases

Shower bases with the seat moulded into the base — no add-on bench, no bracket to mount, and no seam to seal. Seat heights of 15 in. and 18 in. across the Wave, Elite, Contour, Arcadia, and Victory lines.

Contour adds an extra large 18 in. transfer seat and a curved front that opens up more room to shower in, while still fitting a standard alcove.

Product image — Seated Bases
Accessibility

Accessible Bathing

Triumph surrounds are reinforced for grab bar installation, with matching back panels in 54 in. and 60 in. and side panels in 27 in., 30 in., and 32 in. They are ADA compliant when the grab bars are installed as shown.

Select ADA is built around a 1 in. barrier free threshold, so the shower can be entered without a step up.

Product image — Accessible Bathing
Adjustable

Glue-Up Surrounds

Solid ABS panels that carry no sprayed fiberglass, so they can be cut on site to fit the base already in the room. Eight models, and one panel can cover as many as six sizes.

They bond to the wall that is already there, so nothing comes down to the studs and nothing goes in a dumpster.
