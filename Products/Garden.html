Room Enough to Stretch Out

Garden runs 42 in. deep in a 54 in. alcove, which is deeper than a standard tub opening. The line covers two bathtubs holding up to 65 gallons, a shower base, and the surround that goes over them.

Up to 65 Gallons

The two Garden bathtubs hold 63 and 65 gallons.

10-1/2 in. Foot Rests

The shower base carries moulded foot rests for washing and shaving.

A Removable Panel

A removable panel gives access after the bathtub is set.

Made in Michigan

We mould, spray, and pack every part in our own Dowagiac plant.

Bathtubs

A Long Soak in a Wide Tub

Two Garden bathtubs share the 54 in. by 42 in. footprint and hold 63 and 65 gallons.

Both use a center drain, suit an above floor rough, and are reinforced with fiberglass and composite materials.

Product image — A Long Soak in a Wide Tub
Base and Surround

A Shower on the Same Footprint

The Garden shower base matches the bathtub footprint and adds 10-1/2 in. foot rests and a slip resistant floor.

The surround carries six 3 in. shelves and fits most garden bathtubs and bases.
